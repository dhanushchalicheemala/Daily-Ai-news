# AI News — July 31, 2026

## Startups & Funding

- **Assort Health raises $120M Series C at $1.2B valuation** — The healthcare AI agents platform, which has powered more than 190M patient voice interactions (scheduling, intake, referrals, forms, payments), closed a round led by Menlo Ventures with Lightspeed, Felicis, First Round Capital and others. Total funding now exceeds $222M, and the company says revenue has grown 20x in the last 15 months. [Assort Health blog](https://www.assorthealth.com/blog/assort-health-raises-120-million-series-c-to-scale-largest-deployment-of-ai-agents-for-the-patient-journey) · [FierceHealthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/assort-health-scores-120m-series-c-scale-voice-ai-agent-platform-healthcare)
- **North American startup funding hits record highs in H1 2026, driven by AI** — US and Canadian startups raised $392B in the first half of 2026, a record, with nearly 88% of AI-related funding ($319B) flowing to US-headquartered companies. Capital remains heavily concentrated in foundation models, AI infrastructure, and a small circle of companies including OpenAI and Anthropic. [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/na-startup-funding-ma-shattered-records-ai-q2-2026/)

## IPOs & Corporate Moves

- **AMD and Anthropic announce up to $5B strategic partnership** — Announced July 22, the deal has Anthropic deploying up to 2 gigawatts of AMD Instinct MI450-series GPUs starting in H1 2027, with AMD committing up to $5B in equity investment released as deployment milestones are hit. The companies will also collaborate on using Claude models to optimize workloads for AMD hardware and accelerate ROCm software development. [AMD Newsroom](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-anthropic-strategic-partnership/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/22/amd-anthropic-ai-chip-investment.html)
- **OpenAI and Anthropic reportedly on track for public market debuts later in 2026** — Both companies remain the top two recipients of AI startup funding, per Crunchbase reporting on H1 2026 investment trends. [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/us-ai-startup-funding-boom-data/)

## New Models & Tools

- **Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5** — Released July 24 and available across all platforms, Opus 5 targets performance close to Claude's most capable model at roughly half the price of comparable tiers, with a Fast mode running ~2.5x the default speed at 2x the base price. It's now the default model on Claude Max and the top option on Claude Pro. Anthropic also introduced beta features for mid-conversation tool changes (without invalidating prompt cache) and automatic safety-classifier fallbacks on the API. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-launches-opus-5/) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-releases-new-model-opus-5)
- **Meituan releases LongCat-2.0, an open-source 1.6T-parameter model for agentic coding** — The model uses sparse attention and N-gram embedding innovations, with about 48B active parameters, aimed specifically at complex agentic coding workloads. [AI Agent Store weekly roundup](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week)
- **A busy month of frontier releases continues to settle in** — Following GPT-5.6 (Sol/Terra/Luna variants), Grok 4.5, and Claude Sonnet 5 earlier in July, open-weight models GLM-5.2, DeepSeek V4 and Qwen 3.6 also launched, broadening the competitive open-source field alongside the frontier labs. [ThursdAI July 2026 releases roundup](https://thursdai.news/releases/2026-07)
