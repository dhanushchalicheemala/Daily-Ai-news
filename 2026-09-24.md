## Breakthroughs & Research

- **Anthropic's Claude discovers a novel CRISPR-like enzyme system** — Anthropic's new life-sciences research lab reported that Claude, working autonomously across roughly 950 agents over 21 hours (about 210 million tokens), searched a DNA database of reverse transcriptases, screened over 200,000 candidates, and identified a previously uncharacterized system — dubbed "array-associated reverse transcriptases" (ART) — found mainly in bacteriophages, whose non-coding DNA repeats resemble the arrays behind CRISPR gene editing. CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang (MIT/Broad Institute) called the finding "genuinely intriguing" and worth further investigation. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system)
