## Breakthroughs & Research

- **Claude formalizes a full computer-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem** — Working largely autonomously over 11 days on the Prove2Me platform (built by Tianyi Peng and collaborators at Columbia), dozens of parallel Claude agents generated 13 million lines of Lean code and proved 30,300 theorems (29,500 used in the final proof), consuming roughly 6 billion output tokens. Imperial College London mathematician Kevin Buzzard, who reviewed the proof, called it an "extraordinary autoformalization achievement" resting on nothing but the axioms of mathematics — work that took human experts months to verify when Andrew Wiles first published it. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem) ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/04/anthropic-uses-claude-to-formalize-proof-of-fermats-last-theorem/))
