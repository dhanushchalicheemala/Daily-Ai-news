## Breakthroughs & Research

- **AI-designed vaccine clears first human trials** — University of Cambridge researchers reported that a vaccine whose key component was designed entirely by AI has completed initial human trials — the first time an AI-designed vaccine component has reached human testing, pointing toward AI-driven design of vaccines against diverse viral targets. [Crescendo AI](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)
- **Photonic AI chip matches expert-level medical diagnostics** — A team led by Professor Han Zhang at Shenzhen University built an all-fiber photonic AI platform using black-phosphorus tunable modulators that processes medical images with light instead of electrons, reportedly reaching expert-level accuracy on retinal detachment and liver cancer diagnosis at roughly 246x the efficiency of conventional GPUs. [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/computers_math/artificial_intelligence/)
- **Penn researchers build light-matter particles for faster AI compute** — Researchers at the University of Pennsylvania created a hybrid light-matter particle they say could substantially speed up AI computing while cutting energy use, an early-stage step toward more efficient hardware for AI workloads. [Crescendo AI](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)
