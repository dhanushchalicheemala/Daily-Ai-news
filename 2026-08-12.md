## Breakthroughs & Research

- **Anthropic begins watermarking Claude output to meet EU rules** — Following the August 2 start of enforcement under the EU AI Act's Transparency Code, Anthropic said all Claude models released after that date will automatically watermark AI-generated text (imperceptibly, persisting through copy-paste and some editing) and files via the C2PA standard, across the Claude app, API, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and Claude Tag.
