## Breakthroughs & Research

- **DeepMind addresses "reasoning collapse" in LLMs** — New research targets a failure mode where models that solve a problem correctly in one framing fail on an equivalent problem stated differently; the proposed training methodology reportedly cuts this brittleness by roughly 40%. [Source](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026)
- **AI-guided search speeds alloy discovery for NASA** — Researchers used an AI system to search over 100 million possible 3D-printing configurations for a high-performance NASA alloy, identifying six successful settings after just 40 physical experiments. [Source](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026)
- **Research focus shifts toward reliability and efficiency** — August's research output shows growing emphasis on reliability over raw capability, AI applied directly to scientific discovery, efficiency as a first-class design constraint, and maturing safety-evaluation methodology. [Source](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026)
