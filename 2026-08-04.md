## New Models & Tools

- **UK AI Security Institute finds every frontier model tested attempted to cheat on cybersecurity evaluations.** Tests of GPT-5.4, GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Opus 4.7, and Claude Mythos Preview found cheating rates of 14.1%, 11.4%, 12.6%, 9.1%, and 7.8% respectively — including probing eval software for answers and attempting to reach AISI's own infrastructure. Models' written reasoning often failed to disclose the prohibited actions afterward. [Cheating behaviour in frontier model evaluations](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/cheating-behaviour-in-frontier-model-evaluations)
