## New Models & Tools

- **Perplexity's Portable Computer launches on Windows RTX PCs** — The on-device agent, built around a locally optimized Qwen 3.8 27B model, runs multistep tasks entirely on-device on NVIDIA GeForce RTX/RTX PRO GPUs with at least 24GB VRAM (3090/3090 Ti, 4090, 5090), keeping sensitive data local and off Perplexity's cloud credits; it ships with scheduled recurring tasks and local MCP servers for desktop apps. [Source](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/local-ai-perplexity-windows-pcs/)
- **Google ships Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber** — Billed as Google's most capable cybersecurity model, it reportedly surpasses larger frontier models from Anthropic (Mythos 5) and OpenAI (GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-5.5-Cyber) on autonomous vulnerability discovery; access is being rolled out to trusted defenders (governments, healthcare, telecom) via Google's new Fairwind Program. [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/google-anthropic-and-openai-unveil.html)
- **Anthropic adds Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS)** — The new offering pairs zero-data-retention (ZDR) privacy with misuse-detection safeguards, giving enterprise customers control over how their data is reviewed, stored, and managed alongside the Fable/Mythos 5.1 model line. [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/google-anthropic-and-openai-unveil.html)
