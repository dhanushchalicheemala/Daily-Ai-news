## Breakthroughs & Research

- **MIT's "HardFlow" targets safety-critical generative AI** — The new algorithm enforces hard constraints only on a model's final output rather than at every intermediate step, letting pretrained generative models satisfy strict safety, physical or task requirements without retraining — validated in robotics, physical-process control and computer vision. Published in IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence. [Source](https://news.mit.edu/2026/new-method-enables-ai-safety-critical-situations-0914)
- **AI research assistants keep gaining ground** — Coverage continues to track the field's shift from chat tools toward research partners, workflow operators, lab assistants and robot controllers, with embodied AI systems showing improved whole-body reasoning and task planning in warehouses, labs, farms and manufacturing. [Source](https://blog.mean.ceo/latest-ai-breakthroughs-news-september-2026/)
