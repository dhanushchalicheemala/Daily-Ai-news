## New Models & Tools

- **xAI launches Grok 4.6** — xAI released Grok 4.6 on August 12, a flagship multimodal model tuned for long-running agents, coding, and knowledge work, with a 500K-token context window and a new "xhigh" reasoning-effort tier. It scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, matching GPT-5.6 Sol and priced at roughly half the rate of comparable frontier models ($2/$6 per million input/output tokens). It's available via Cursor, Grok Build, and API. [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/12/spacexai-releases-grok-4-6/) · [Basenor](https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/xai-launches-grok-4-6-1753-elo-half-the-price-of-rival-frontier-models)
- **Meta debuts Muse Code coding agent** — Meta Superintelligence Labs launched Muse Code, a terminal-based coding agent for macOS and Linux built on the new Muse Spark 1.2 model. It runs persistent background agents that retain context across a session, planning changes, writing code, and validating results across large repositories. Standard pricing is $1.25/$4.25 per million input/output tokens, with a discounted "contributor tier" for users who opt in to data sharing. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/meta-debuts-muse-code-to-take-on-anthropic-and-openai-.html) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/08/06/meta-launches-muse-code-a-new-ai-coding-agent-powered-by-spark-12/)
- **Small open-weight models keep getting more capable per GB** — Coverage this week highlights that 27-billion-parameter open models now run comfortably on consumer hardware (roughly 17GB of combined RAM/VRAM), continuing the trend of frontier-adjacent capability becoming runnable locally on a single high-end GPU or unified-memory Mac. [Local AI Zone](https://local-ai-zone.github.io/blog/ai-updates-august-2026.html)

## Breakthroughs & Research

- **DARPA flies AI-piloted F-16 in real-world flight test** — The U.S. Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency completed the first real-world flight of an F-16 fighter jet fully controlled by AI with no human pilot aboard, a milestone for autonomous combat aviation. [imfounder](https://imfounder.com/science-tech/ai/ai-updates-august-2026-openai-astra-deepmind/)
- **DeepMind targets "reasoning collapse" in LLMs** — Google DeepMind published research on reasoning collapse, where models that solve a problem correctly under one phrasing fail on a logically equivalent restatement. The team's proposed training methodology reportedly cuts this brittleness by roughly 40% on their internal benchmark. [Skycrumbs](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026)
- **Meta AI Research advances video "temporal grounding"** — A new Meta AI Research paper addresses temporal grounding, improving a video-generation model's ability to keep physical properties and character identity consistent across longer generated clips. [Skycrumbs](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026)
