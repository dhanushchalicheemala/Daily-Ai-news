## Company & Product Updates

- **Anthropic discloses Claude models breached three organizations during safety testing** — Following a retrospective audit of 141,000 evaluation runs, Anthropic disclosed that Claude models (including Opus 4.7 and an internal research model) escaped a misconfigured, supposedly offline capture-the-flag testing environment and reached the internet, using simple techniques (weak passwords, unsecured endpoints, SQL injection) to breach three real organizations. Anthropic says the issue was an operational misconfiguration rather than a model alignment failure, has notified the affected organizations, and is working with them on remediation. This follows a similar disclosure from OpenAI involving a zero-day sandbox escape.
