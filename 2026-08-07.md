## IPOs & Corporate Moves

- **Google is consolidating its AI leadership in Mountain View, California**, appointing Koray Kavukcuoglu to run its AI research and operations; DeepMind co-founder Demis Hassabis has stepped back from day-to-day duties to become chairman of Google DeepMind and Alphabet's Chief Scientist. Sebastian Borgeaud, who leads a key AI coding effort, also relocated from the UK to California. The move comes amid researcher departures to rivals Anthropic and OpenAI. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/google-shifts-ai-power-to-california-in-race-against-anthropic-openai)
- Google has pledged up to $205B in capital expenditures this year to support its AI ambitions, underscoring the scale of the compute arms race against OpenAI and Anthropic. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-06/google-s-shakeup-complicates-race-with-openai-anthropic)
- Staff from **OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, and Thinking Machines** co-signed a letter urging the U.S. government to support international technical/governance mechanisms that could slow frontier AI development if needed, citing concerns about recursive self-improvement (RSI). [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-fearing-rsi-openai-anthropic)

## Breakthroughs & Research

- Researchers at **Shenzhen University** developed an all-fiber photonic AI platform for medical diagnostics that processes medical images using light instead of electrons, reaching expert-level accuracy for retinal detachment and liver cancer diagnosis while running 246x more efficiently than conventional GPUs. [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/computers_math/artificial_intelligence/)
- **University of Cambridge** researchers announced an AI-designed vaccine has completed its first human trials — the first time a vaccine's key component was designed entirely by AI. [Crescendo AI](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)
- **University of Michigan** researchers built an AI system that interprets brain MRI scans in seconds, accurately identifying a wide range of neurological conditions. [Crescendo AI](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)
- **UC San Diego and the Allen Institute for AI** developed Spherical DYffusion, a generative-plus-physics model that projects 100 years of climate patterns in 25 hours — 25x faster than current methods. [Crescendo AI](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)
- **Penn** researchers created a hybrid light-matter particle (a polariton-based device) that could dramatically speed up AI computing while cutting energy use. [Crescendo AI](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)
