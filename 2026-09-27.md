## Breakthroughs & Research

- **Anthropic's Claude improves a 50-year-old Riemann zeta function bound** — An unreleased research version of Claude pushed the proven lower bound for the fraction of zeta-function zeros lying on the critical line from 41.6% to 67.2%, a genuine advance on a decades-old problem (though not a proof of the Riemann hypothesis itself). The result was validated internally by Anthropic mathematicians Levent Alpöge and Ralph Furman, reviewed externally by Brian Conrey and Dan Goldston, and formalized in Lean; Oxford's James Maynard called it "a genuinely interesting mathematical contribution." [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta)
